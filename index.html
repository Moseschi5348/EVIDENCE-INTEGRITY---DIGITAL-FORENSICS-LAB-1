03 / INTERPRET WITH CARE

A log is a lead,
not a verdict.

The records show events attributed to accounts and sessions; they do not prove who controlled them, why the changes occurred, that money moved, or that the simulated email was authentic. MFA is recorded as NOT_CAPTURED. Log completeness, clock accuracy, and the identity behind each action remain unverified.

Preserve and hash the source exports and configuration snapshots. Obtain the original identity, portal, proxy, mail, and change-management records before drawing conclusions. If handling real records, follow applicable Nigerian evidence and data-protection requirements.
